Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to Work & Income Pakistan. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how third-party services may handle information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

Our website provides informational resources about online earning, employment opportunities, skills and career development. Visitors do not need to create an account to browse our website.

We may receive basic technical and usage information through website hosting services and analytics tools. This may include browser type, device information, pages visited and general website usage data.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to share, such as your email address and the contents of your message. This information is used to respond to your enquiry.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Maintain and improve our website and its content.
  • Understand visitor interests and website performance.
  • Respond to messages and enquiries sent to us.
  • Protect the website against misuse and security problems.
  • Understand how visitors interact with our resources and links.

3. Cookies and Google Analytics

Our website and third-party services may use cookies and similar technologies to understand website activity, measure performance and support certain features.

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use our website, which pages they visit and how the website performs. This information helps us improve our content and visitor experience.

Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, browser and device information, and general usage data through cookies and similar technologies.

Google processes information according to its own privacy documentation. You can read more here: Google Privacy Policy .

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website or third-party features may not work as expected if certain cookies are disabled.

4. Affiliate Links and Tracking

Some product or service links on Work & Income Pakistan are affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make an eligible purchase, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate partners may use tracking links, cookies or similar technologies to identify referrals and attribute eligible purchases or commissions. Any information collected through those technologies is handled according to the relevant partner's privacy policy.

When you visit an external affiliate website, its own privacy policy and terms apply.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

We may use Google AdSense or other advertising services in the future. If advertising is introduced, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalise where applicable, and measure advertisements.

Google's advertising practices and available privacy controls are explained in its documentation: Google Advertising and Privacy Information .

6. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external job platforms, educational resources, product sellers, affiliate partners and other service providers.

When you visit an external website or use a third-party service, that provider may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. We do not control the privacy practices of external websites.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before sharing personal information or making a purchase.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of our website. However, no website or online service can guarantee absolute security. Visitors should avoid sharing passwords, financial details or other sensitive information through unsecured channels.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website provides general informational resources and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or applicable requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page, along with a revised update date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact us through our Contact Us page.

How to Prepare a Professional CV in Pakistan

A CV (Curriculum Vitae) is a document that introduces your education, skills, experience and qualifications to an employer. A clear and well-organised CV can help employers understand your suitability for a job.

1. Add Your Contact Information

Start with the essential details:

  • Your full name.
  • A professional email address.
  • Your phone number with country code.
  • Your city and country.
  • A professional LinkedIn profile, if available.

Never include passwords, bank details or unnecessary personal information.

2. Write a Short Professional Summary

Write two or three sentences explaining your experience, strongest skills and career goals. Keep the information honest and relevant to the job.

3. Include Your Work Experience

For each relevant job, mention:

  • Your job title.
  • Company or employer name.
  • Employment dates.
  • Your main duties and achievements.

If you have no formal work experience, include relevant volunteer work, internships, personal projects or practical training where appropriate.

4. Add Your Education

Mention your school, college, university or training qualifications, along with the institution and completion year where relevant.

5. Highlight Your Skills

Include skills that genuinely match the job, such as:

  • Computer and office software skills.
  • Communication and customer service.
  • Writing and research.
  • Graphic design or video editing.
  • Driving or technical skills, where relevant.
  • Languages you can actually communicate in.

6. Keep Your CV Easy to Read

  • Use clear headings and consistent formatting.
  • Choose a simple, readable font.
  • Use bullet points instead of long paragraphs.
  • Check spelling and grammar.
  • Keep information relevant to the position.
  • Follow the employer's requested file format.

7. Tailor Your CV to Each Job

Read the job description carefully. Highlight the skills and experience that match the employer's requirements. Do not use the same generic CV for every application.

8. Avoid Common CV Mistakes

  • Never invent qualifications or work experience.
  • Avoid spelling and formatting mistakes.
  • Do not include irrelevant personal details.
  • Use a professional email address.
  • Check your contact information before applying.

Free Tools to Create a CV

You can explore these resources:

  • Canva Resume Templates
  • Google Docs

Final CV Checklist

  • Contact details are correct.
  • Education and experience are accurate.
  • Skills match the job.
  • Spelling and grammar are checked.
  • The document is saved in the requested format.

Conclusion

A professional CV does not need complicated designs. It needs accurate information, clear presentation and relevant skills. Update your CV whenever you gain new experience or qualifications.
